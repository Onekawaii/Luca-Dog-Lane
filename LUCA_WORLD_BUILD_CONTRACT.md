# Luca Dog World — Build Contract

## Canonical prompt

> Build this entire world like the hive lattice. With the rooms but make it an entire world with hills and climbable mountains with cars to drive and free roam with 10 different biomes and 20 different types of levels that at fully walkable and explorable. Build me an entire fully working apk And PC version

## Interpretation

This repository's Hive-Lattice systems are the engine/substrate, not the identity of the finished game.
The shipped game is Luca Dog World.
Existing open-world, room, procedural, vehicle, climbing, persistence, mobile, PC, test, and release infrastructure must be preserved unless replacement is required by the Luca game.
Generic Arkheopantheochive/Hive world content must not silently substitute for Luca-world content.

## Originality constraint

The Pokémon concept is removed. Do not use Pokémon branding, terminology, capture mechanics, gyms, trainers, Poké Balls, type charts, or imitation creature/evolution systems.
Creatures, ecology, progression, encounters, companions, lore, and world rules must be original to Luca Dog World.

## Required playable scope

- Luca Dog World is the player-facing game identity.
- A continuous free-roam world with rooms/interiors integrated into the landscape.
- Hills and climbable mountains, drivable cars, exactly 10 explorable biomes, and at least 20 fully walkable/explorable level or location types.
- Existing authored rooms remain usable as world interiors/anchors where they fit Luca World.
- Android APK and Windows PC build are required release artifacts.
- Headless verification is necessary but does not count as visual/playable acceptance.
- No release is complete until the built game visibly identifies as Luca Dog World and the Luca content layer is present.
